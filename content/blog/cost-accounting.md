# 1. Family Budgeting and Saving for a Used Car

A married couple with three school-aged children are planning their finances at the beginning of the year. They want to calculate how long it will take to save enough money to purchase a used car costing Rp 95,000,000.

Their current financial situation is as follows:

Monthly income

- Husband's net salary: Rp 6,000,000
- Wife's net salary: Rp 4,500,000
- An annual bonus (THR) equal to one month's salary will be received in the fourth month of the year.
- Current joint savings balance: Rp 35,000,000.

Monthly expenses

- Household expenses: Rp 4,000,000
- First child's school expenses: Rp 700,000
- Second child's school expenses: Rp 650,000
- Third child's school expenses: Rp 500,000
- Parents' routine medical expenses: Rp 500,000
- Miscellaneous and unexpected expenses: Rp 1,000,000

Known upcoming annual expenses

- Month 4: School enrollment fee for the first child, who is moving from junior high school to senior high school: Rp 1,800,000.
- Month 6: Childbirth expenses for the wife, budgeted at Rp 6,000,000 for a normal delivery.
- Starting in month 6: An additional Rp 550,000 per month for the fourth child's expenses.

The family plans to save their money starting now to buy the used car.

### Questions

1. In which month will they achieve their goal of purchasing the car, assuming all the stated budget amounts are spent?
2. If miscellaneous and unexpected expenses can be reduced by 50%, how many months earlier can they purchase the car?

# 2. Pineapple and Strawberry Jam Production

"NATURE GOODNESS" processes fresh pineapples and fresh strawberries into jam sold in 400-gram bottles under the brand name "SWEETNESS"

The following information applies to July 2022.

A. Production output

- Pineapple jam: 1,750 bottles
- Strawberry jam: 2,250 bottles

B. Raw material composition for 250 g of pineapple jam

| Ingredient       | Quantity                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Pineapple        | 1 fruit weighing 625 g          |
| Granulated sugar | 100 g                           |
| Palm sugar       | 60 g                            |
| Cinnamon         | 2 g                             |
| Dried cloves     | 1 clove (4 dried cloves = 1 g)  |
| Salt             | 1/8 teaspoon (1 teaspoon = 4 g) |

C. Raw material composition for 500 g of strawberry jam

| Ingredient       | Quantity                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Strawberries     | 0.5 kg                                |
| Granulated sugar | 0.5 kg                                |
| Lemon            | 1/2 fruit (1 kg of lemons = 5 fruits) |

D. Purchase prices of raw materials

| Ingredient       | Purchase price |
| ---------------- | -------------- |
| Pineapple        | Rp 12,000/kg   |
| Granulated sugar | Rp 18,000/kg   |
| Palm sugar       | Rp 30,000/kg   |
| Cinnamon         | Rp 121,875/kg  |
| Dried cloves     | Rp 95,000/kg   |
| Salt             | Rp 7,500/500 g |
| Strawberries     | Rp 94,000/kg   |
| Lemons           | Rp 75,000/kg   |

E. Packaging costs and waste

Packaging consists of glass bottles with aluminum caps and labels in the form of stickers.

- Glass bottle with aluminum cap: Rp 9,500 each.
- Label: Rp 50 each.
- During July 2022, 2% of the bottles were damaged. The cost of these damaged bottles is allocated proportionally according to the number of bottles used for pineapple and strawberry jam.
- Damaged labels account for 6% of pineapple jam labels and 4% of strawberry jam labels. These costs are charged to the respective products.

F. Production departments and actual costs

Production takes place through two departments: the Processing Department and the Packaging Department. The indirect production departments consist of the Inspection Division and the Production Administration Division.

Actual costs for July 2022:

| Department              | Actual cost   |
| ----------------------- | ------------- |
| Processing Division     | Rp 14,835,000 |
| Packaging Division      | Rp 10,345,250 |
| Inspection Division     | Rp 12,470,000 |
| Administration Division | Rp 4,730,000  |

G. Allocation of direct production department costs

Direct production department costs are allocated to the two products based on production quantities. The allocation value for pineapple jam is 30% higher than that for strawberry jam because strawberry jam is more difficult to process.

H. Indirect production costs

Budgeted indirect production costs are:

- Pineapple jam: Rp 4,000 per bottle.
- Strawberry jam: Rp 4,200 per bottle.

The difference between actual and budgeted indirect production costs is allocated to the two products based on production quantities, using a ratio of 49:51.

All jam produced in July 2022 was sold at the following prices:

- Pineapple jam: Rp 65,000 per bottle.
- Strawberry jam: Rp 105,000 per bottle.

### Questions

Calculate the following for July 2022:

a. The raw material cost for 1,750 bottles of pineapple jam, both in total and per bottle.

b. The raw material cost for 2,250 bottles of strawberry jam, both in total and per bottle.

c. The packaging cost for pineapple and strawberry jam, both in total and per bottle.

d. The direct production cost for pineapple and strawberry jam, both in total and per bottle.

e. The indirect production cost for pineapple and strawberry jam, both in total and per bottle.

# 3. Superbread Production

The bakery "GREAT TASTE" sells its homemade bread, called "SUPERBREAD" In January 2022, it produced 330,000 pieces of bread, each weighing 8 grams.

The following information applies.

A. Raw materials

| Ingredient     | Composition | Purchase price      |
| -------------- | ----------- | ------------------- |
| Flour          | 45%         | Rp 26,000 per 500 g |
| Sugar          | 15%         | Rp 18,000 per kg    |
| Eggs           | 10%         | Rp 24,000 per kg    |
| Sliced almonds | 10%         | Rp 22,000 per 100 g |
| Butter         | 20%         | Rp 31,000 per 400 g |

A 4% production loss means that every 100 g of raw dough ingredients produces only 96 g of finished bread.

B. Supporting materials

Supporting ingredients include baking powder, vanilla, food coloring, and other similar ingredients.

- Supporting material cost: Rp 7,500 per kg of bread.

C. Packaging materials

Bread is packaged in plastic bags.

- Price of plastic: Rp 24,000 per kg.
- One kilogram of plastic produces 5,000 bags.
- Each bread uses one bag.
- Plastic waste is 4%, meaning that 4% of the plastic is damaged.

D. Labor costs

There are four employees, whose salaries are calculated based on a monthly minimum wage (UMR) of Rp 4,300,000:

- Three dough makers, each earning 0.80 times the minimum wage.
- One supervisor, earning 1.00 times the minimum wage.

The minimum production target is 270,000 pieces of bread per month. Production above this target earns bonuses:

- Dough makers: Rp 32.50 per additional bread.
- Supervisor: Rp 29 per additional bread.

E. Indirect production costs

- Electricity and water: Rp 5,250 per kg of raw materials.
- Cleaning and security: Rp 891,000 per month.
- Building, equipment, and other maintenance: 60% of electricity and water costs.

All 330,000 pieces of bread were sold out in January 2022 at Rp 2,000 per piece.

### Questions

a. Calculate the raw material cost for 330,000 pieces of bread, both in total and per piece.

b. Calculate the supporting material cost for 330,000 pieces of bread, both in total and per piece.

c. Calculate the packaging material cost for 330,000 pieces of bread, both in total and per piece.

d. Calculate the difference between the total earnings of each dough maker and the supervisor. Also, calculate the labor cost per piece of bread.

e. Calculate the total indirect production costs for January 2022, both in total and per piece of bread.

# Answer

## Family Budgeting

### Question 1: When can they buy the car?

Monthly income:

6,000,000 + 4,500,000 = 10,500,000

Monthly expenses for months 1–5, excluding the one-time school fee:

4,000,000 + 700,000 + 650,000 + 500,000 + 500,000 + 1,000,000 = 7,350,000

Monthly savings before the fourth child is born:

10,500,000 − 7,350,000 = 3,150,000

| Month | Calculation | Ending savings |
|---|---|---:|
| Starting balance | — | Rp 35,000,000 |
| 1 | + Rp 3,150,000 | Rp 38,150,000 |
| 2 | + Rp 3,150,000 | Rp 41,300,000 |
| 3 | + Rp 3,150,000 | Rp 44,450,000 |
| 4 | + Rp 3,150,000 + Rp 10,500,000 THR − Rp 1,800,000 | Rp 56,300,000 |
| 5 | + Rp 3,150,000 | Rp 59,450,000 |
| 6 | + Rp 2,600,000 − Rp 6,000,000 childbirth | Rp 56,050,000 |

From month 7 onward, monthly savings are:

10,500,000 − (7,350,000 + 550,000) = 2,600,000

After month 20, savings reach Rp 92,450,000. In month 21, they reach Rp 95,050,000.

#### Answer 1: Month 21.

### Question 2: What if miscellaneous expenses are reduced by 50%?

The monthly miscellaneous expense falls from Rp 1,000,000 to Rp 500,000.

From month 7 onward, savings become:

10,500,000 − 7,400,000 = 3,100,000

After month 17, savings are Rp 93,150,000. In month 18, they reach Rp 96,250,000.

#### Answer 2: Month 18, which is 3 months earlier.

## Jam Production

The company produces 1,750 bottles of pineapple jam and 2,250 bottles of strawberry jam in July 2022. Each bottle contains 400 g of jam.

### (a) Raw material costs

#### Pineapple jam

The recipe produces 250 g of jam, so the ingredient costs must be scaled to 400 g.

| Ingredient       | Calculation            | Cost      |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- | --------- |
| Pineapple        | 0.625 kg × Rp 12,000   | Rp 7,500  |
| Granulated sugar | 0.1 kg × Rp 18,000     | Rp 1,800  |
| Palm sugar       | 0.06 kg × Rp 30,000    | Rp 1,800  |
| Cinnamon         | 0.002 kg × Rp 121,875  | Rp 243.75 |
| Dried cloves     | 0.00025 kg × Rp 95,000 | Rp 23.75  |
| Salt             | 0.0005 kg × Rp 15,000  | Rp 7.50   |
| Total for 250 g  |                        | Rp 11,375 |

Cost per 400 g bottle:

11,375 × (400 ÷ 250) = Rp 18,200

Total raw material cost:

1,750 × Rp 18,200 = Rp 31,850,000

#### Strawberry jam

The recipe produces 500 g of jam.

| Ingredient       | Calculation        | Cost      |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | --------- |
| Strawberries     | 0.5 kg × Rp 94,000 | Rp 47,000 |
| Granulated sugar | 0.5 kg × Rp 18,000 | Rp 9,000  |
| Lemon            | 0.1 kg × Rp 75,000 | Rp 7,500  |
| Total for 500 g  |                    | Rp 63,500 |

Cost per 400 g bottle:

63,500 × (400 ÷ 500) = Rp 50,800

Total raw material cost:

2,250 × Rp 50,800 = Rp 114,300,000

#### Answer (a)

| Product        | Total raw material cost | Cost per bottle |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | --------------- |
| Pineapple jam  | Rp 31,850,000           | Rp 18,200       |
| Strawberry jam | Rp 114,300,000          | Rp 50,800       |

### (b) Packaging costs

Packaging consists of glass bottles with aluminum caps costing Rp 9,500 each and labels costing Rp 50 each.

Assuming damaged bottles require 2% additional packaging and damaged labels require 6% additional labels for pineapple jam and 4% for strawberry jam:

Pineapple jam

- Bottle cost per bottle: Rp 9,500 × 1.02 = Rp 9,690
- Label cost per bottle: Rp 50 × 1.06 = Rp 53
- Total packaging cost per bottle: Rp 9,743
- Total packaging cost: 1,750 × Rp 9,743 = Rp 17,050,250

Strawberry jam

- Bottle cost per bottle: Rp 9,500 × 1.02 = Rp 9,690
- Label cost per bottle: Rp 50 × 1.04 = Rp 52
- Total packaging cost per bottle: Rp 9,742
- Total packaging cost: 2,250 × Rp 9,742 = Rp 21,919,500

#### Answer (b)

| Product        | Total packaging cost | Cost per bottle |
| -------------- | -------------------- | --------------- |
| Pineapple jam  | Rp 17,050,250        | Rp 9,743        |
| Strawberry jam | Rp 21,919,500        | Rp 9,742        |

### (c) Direct production costs

Actual costs for the two direct production departments are:

- Processing Division: Rp 14,835,000
- Packaging Division: Rp 10,345,250

Total direct production department costs:

14,835,000 + 10,345,250 = Rp 25,180,250

The question states that the allocation value per bottle for pineapple jam is 30% higher than for strawberry jam.

Allocation weights:

- Pineapple jam: 1,750 × 1.3 = 2,275
- Strawberry jam: 2,250 × 1.0 = 2,250

Total allocation weight = 4,525.

Pineapple jam allocation:

25,180,250 × (2,275 ÷ 4,525) ≈ Rp 12,659,684

Strawberry jam allocation:

25,180,250 × (2,250 ÷ 4,525) ≈ Rp 12,520,566

#### Answer (c)

| Product        | Total direct production cost | Cost per bottle |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------- |
| Pineapple jam  | Rp 12,659,684                | Rp 7,234.10     |
| Strawberry jam | Rp 12,520,566                | Rp 5,564.70     |

### (d) Indirect production costs

Actual indirect production costs:

- Inspection Division: Rp 12,470,000
- Administration Division: Rp 4,730,000

Total actual indirect costs:

12,470,000 + 4,730,000 = Rp 17,200,000

Budgeted indirect costs:

- Pineapple jam: 1,750 × Rp 4,000 = Rp 7,000,000
- Strawberry jam: 2,250 × Rp 4,200 = Rp 9,450,000

Total budgeted indirect costs = Rp 16,450,000.

Unfavorable variance:

17,200,000 − 16,450,000 = Rp 750,000

The variance is allocated in a 49:51 ratio.

- Pineapple jam: Rp 750,000 × 49% = Rp 367,500
- Strawberry jam: Rp 750,000 × 51% = Rp 382,500

#### Answer (d)

| Product        | Budgeted cost | Variance allocation | Total allocated cost | Cost per bottle |
| -------------- | ------------- | ------------------- | -------------------- | --------------- |
| Pineapple jam  | Rp 7,000,000  | Rp 367,500          | Rp 7,367,500         | Rp 4,210        |
| Strawberry jam | Rp 9,450,000  | Rp 382,500          | Rp 9,832,500         | Rp 4,370        |

## Bread Production

The bakery produces 330,000 pieces of bread per month. Each piece weighs 8 g, and production loss is 4%.

### (a) Raw material costs

Total finished bread weight:

330,000 × 8 g = 2,640,000 g = 2,640 kg.

Because 4% of the raw dough is lost during production, the required raw dough is:

2,640 ÷ 0.96 = 2,750 kg

The ingredient composition is flour 45%, sugar 15%, eggs 10%, sliced almonds 10%, and butter 20%.

| Ingredient     | Required quantity | Price per kg | Total cost     |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ------------ | -------------- |
| Flour          | 1,237.5 kg        | Rp 52,000    | Rp 64,350,000  |
| Sugar          | 412.5 kg          | Rp 18,000    | Rp 7,425,000   |
| Eggs           | 275 kg            | Rp 24,000    | Rp 6,600,000   |
| Sliced almonds | 275 kg            | Rp 220,000   | Rp 60,500,000  |
| Butter         | 550 kg            | Rp 77,500    | Rp 42,625,000  |
| Total          | 2,750 kg          |              | Rp 181,500,000 |

Cost per piece:

181,500,000 ÷ 330,000 = Rp 550

#### Answer (a)

- Total raw material cost: Rp 181,500,000
- Raw material cost per piece: Rp 550

### (b) Supporting material costs

Supporting materials include baking powder, vanilla, food coloring, and similar ingredients. Their cost is Rp 7,500 per kg of bread.

Using the finished bread weight of 2,640 kg:

2,640 × Rp 7,500 = Rp 19,800,000

Cost per piece:

19,800,000 ÷ 330,000 = Rp 60

#### Answer (b)

- Total supporting material cost: Rp 19,800,000
- Supporting material cost per piece: Rp 60

### (c) Packaging costs

Each bread requires one plastic bag. One kilogram of plastic produces 5,000 bags, and 4% of the plastic is wasted.

Required plastic weight:

330,000 ÷ (5,000 × 0.96) = 68.75 kg

Total packaging cost:

68.75 × Rp 24,000 = Rp 1,650,000

Cost per piece:

1,650,000 ÷ 330,000 = Rp 5

#### Answer (c)

- Total packaging cost: Rp 1,650,000
- Packaging cost per piece: Rp 5

### (d) Labor costs and bonuses

The monthly minimum wage is Rp 4,300,000.

Base salaries:

- Each of the three dough makers: 0.8 × Rp 4,300,000 = Rp 3,440,000
- Supervisor: 1.0 × Rp 4,300,000 = Rp 4,300,000

Production exceeds the target by:

330,000 − 270,000 = 60,000 pieces

Assuming the bonus is paid to each employee for every piece produced above the target:

Each dough maker

Bonus:

60,000 × Rp 32.50 = Rp 1,950,000

Total earnings:

3,440,000 + 1,950,000 = Rp 5,390,000

Supervisor

Bonus:

60,000 × Rp 29 = Rp 1,740,000

Total earnings:

4,300,000 + 1,740,000 = Rp 6,040,000

Difference in earnings:

6,040,000 − 5,390,000 = Rp 650,000

Total labor cost:

(3 × Rp 5,390,000) + Rp 6,040,000 = Rp 22,210,000

Labor cost per piece:

22,210,000 ÷ 330,000 ≈ Rp 67.30

#### Answer (d)

- Each dough maker earns: Rp 5,390,000
- Supervisor earns: Rp 6,040,000
- Difference per dough maker versus supervisor: Rp 650,000
- Total labor cost: Rp 22,210,000
- Labor cost per piece: Rp 67.30

This assumes the bonus is calculated separately for each employee. If the bonus rate applies to the team collectively, the result will be different.

### (e) Indirect production costs

Electricity and water cost Rp 5,250 per kg of raw materials.

Electricity and water:

2,750 × Rp 5,250 = Rp 14,437,500

Cleaning and security cost Rp 891,000 per month.

Maintenance costs equal 60% of electricity and water costs:

14,437,500 × 60% = Rp 8,662,500

Total indirect production costs:

14,437,500 + 891,000 + 8,662,500 = Rp 23,991,000

Indirect cost per piece:

23,991,000 ÷ 330,000 = Rp 72.70

#### Answer (e)

- Electricity and water: Rp 14,437,500
- Cleaning and security: Rp 891,000
- Maintenance: Rp 8,662,500
- Total indirect production cost: Rp 23,991,000
- Indirect production cost per piece: Rp 72.70
