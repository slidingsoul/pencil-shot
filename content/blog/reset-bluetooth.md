I bought a wireless mouse, Nemesis Arkana MQ-50. By my hubris, I decided to throw away the box, including the **manual** right after I unboxed it.

And crap, I did **not** read how to reset the bluetooth connection. I asked Gemini, ChatGPT, Google, and even the seller themselves. Their answer is

1. Forget bluetooth device
2. Choose mode BT1 or BT1
3. Press right + left + *DPI* button to get to pairing mode
4. Connect bluetooth again

Turned out they were all **wrong**. Third step should be

3. Pres right + left + *middle* button to get to pairing mode
